# Wish/Curse Content Brainstorm

Reference doc for the "wholesome trojan horse" greeting site. Sender always sees the same fixed UI. Recipient always sees the same generic "something wholesome is loading" sequence, then lands on a randomly-assigned skin revealing whatever the sender actually picked — from genuinely warm to fully savage.

Every message below is tagged with a spice tier (1-5) so the sender UI can show intensity at a glance before picking:

- 🌶️ 1 — Wholesome, always safe, no sting at all
- 🌶️🌶️ 2 — Playful, low stakes, everyone laughs
- 🌶️🌶️🌶️ 3 — Cheeky, a little sting, still friendly
- 🌶️🌶️🌶️🌶️ 4 — Sharp, real teasing, know your audience
- 🌶️🌶️🌶️🌶️🌶️ 5 — Savage, close-friends-only, thick skin required

**Note on the savage tier:** kept at "exaggerated insult-comedy" (roast-battle style: absurd, over-the-top, impersonal) rather than anything that targets real insecurities, appearance, mental health, identity, or anything a person couldn't laugh off from a friend. That's a deliberate line, not a content gap — happy to push it further in a specific direction if wanted, but avoiding genuinely cruel material given this is meant to go out to whoever a sender picks, including people we can't vouch for the state of mind of.

## 1. Wholesome (🌶️1)

Also doubles as real, sincere messages — not just bait for the curse categories.

1. You are exactly the kind of person the word "good" was invented for.
2. Somewhere out there, someone's day got better just because you exist. Today, that someone is me.
3. Consider this your official reminder that you're doing better than you think.
4. If kindness had a face, it'd probably just steal yours.
5. You're the human equivalent of finding money in an old coat pocket.
6. Just so you know: you're allowed to be proud of yourself today.
7. You make hard days feel a little more survivable. That's not nothing.
8. This is a scientifically unverified but deeply felt notice that you are loved.
9. Sending you a virtual hug — weightless, but fully sincere.
10. You're proof that good people are still out here, doing their thing.

## 2. Genie Wish (🌶️1-2)

Framed as wishes granted, not curses — small, delightful, low-stakes magic.

1. Wish granted: every playlist you make from now on has zero skips.
2. Wish granted: you always find a parking spot exactly when you need one.
3. Wish granted: your phone battery lasts 20% longer than it has any right to.
4. Wish granted: the next package you're waiting on arrives a day early.
5. Wish granted: you get the last good seat, every time, forever.
6. Wish granted: your group chat agrees on a restaurant on the first try.
7. Wish granted: you never again get the shopping cart with the squeaky wheel.
8. Wish granted: the WiFi holds steady through your next important call.
9. Wish granted: you wake up tomorrow one full sleep cycle ahead of schedule.
10. Wish granted: your next haircut is somehow exactly what you pictured.

## 3. Backhanded Compliment (🌶️3)

The hinge category — compliment and insult in the same breath.

1. You have the confidence of someone who's never seen the group photo.
2. Not everyone could pull off that level of "trying their best." Respect.
3. You're proof that a good personality really can carry a person.
4. I'd say you've peaked, but that would require peaking to begin with.
5. Your vibe says "main character," your decisions say "comic relief."
6. You bring so much to the table. Mostly snacks, but still.
7. Iconic, in the sense that no one's quite sure what you're going for.
8. You're one of one. Genuinely, no one else does it quite like this.

## 4. Curse (🌶️2-5, mixed intensity within one list)

Framed as a curse being "cast" — supernatural bad-luck flavor rather than direct insult.

**Mild (🌶️2)**

1. Curse cast: you will always get the shopping cart with the squeaky wheel.
2. Curse cast: your show buffers at the exact wrong moment, forever.
3. Curse cast: you'll always sit down right as your leg falls asleep.
4. Curse cast: autocorrect betrays you at least once a week for the rest of your life.

**Medium (🌶️3-4)**

5. Curse cast: every future group photo, you're mid-blink.
6. Curse cast: you always remember the perfect reply five minutes too late.
7. Curse cast: your card gets declined first, every time, purely for dramatic effect.
8. Curse cast: the ice cream truck plays its song and is never, ever actually near you.

**Savage (🌶️5)**

9. Curse cast: you will spend the rest of your life almost being funny.
10. Curse cast: you will be "so close" to winning something, every time, for the rest of your natural life.
11. Curse cast: somewhere, someone is telling a story about you right now, and it's not the flattering one.
12. Curse cast: you peaked. It already happened. You weren't paying attention.

## 5. Roast (🌶️4-5)

Direct, no mystical framing — for people who explicitly want to get roasted.

1. You have main character energy in a story literally nobody asked to read.
2. You're not lazy, you're just deeply committed to conserving energy for absolutely nothing.
3. If overthinking burned calories, you'd be an athlete.
4. You give "reply guy" energy even in real life conversations.
5. The confidence is unmatched. The evidence supporting it? Still pending.
6. You're what happens when "main character syndrome" meets "side character budget."
7. Some people peak in high school. You're still warming up for that.

## 6. Prophecy (🌶️2-3)

Funny-ominous fortune-telling register.

1. The stars have spoken: you will text "we should hang out soon" at least four more times this year without it happening.
2. It is foretold: you will open the fridge, stare into it, and close it without eating, within the hour.
3. The future holds a mystery Tupperware container in your fridge that no one, not even you, can identify.
4. Prophecy: you will rewatch a show you've already seen instead of starting something new. Tonight.
5. It is written: your "five more minutes" will, once again, become forty.

## 7. Chaos (🌶️3-4)

Absurdist, nonsensical — funny because it means nothing, not because it stings.

1. By the power vested in absolutely no one, I hereby declare it Tuesday energy, permanently, for you.
2. You have been visited by the microwave gremlin. Nothing bad happens. It's just watching.
3. This message has unlocked nothing. Absolutely nothing. Enjoy that feeling.
4. You now have access to zero (0) new abilities. Use them wisely.
5. Legally, this message means nothing. Emotionally, I hope it ruins your whole week in a fun way.

## The "trojan horse" loading copy (recipient always sees this, no matter the category)

**Heading:** "Sending you something good..."

**Rotating status lines** (cycle while the fake progress bar fills):

- "Gathering good vibes..."
- "Sprinkling in some kindness..."
- "Double-checking the universe has time for this..."
- "Wrapping it up with care..."
- "Almost there..."

**Duration:** randomize ~2.5–4s so it doesn't feel scripted, then reveal.

## Recipient skins (pre-designed, randomly assigned, independent of message category)

1. **Confetti Pop** — pastel confetti burst, rounded card, bouncy sans
2. **Starlight** — deep navy, twinkling stars, gold serif
3. **Floral Bloom** — soft florals framing the card, handwritten-style font
4. **Retro Postcard** — cream paper texture, stamp graphic, typewriter font
5. **Balloon Drop** — rising balloons, birthday-party palette
6. **Zen Garden** — muted sage greens, soft circles, calm serif
7. **Neon Arcade** — dark background, neon glow, pixel-style accents
8. **Paper Lantern** — warm amber tones, lantern glow, elegant script

The reveal card can carry a small category badge (💛 Wholesome / 🧞 Wish Granted / 🔮 Cursed / 🔥 Roasted / 🙃 Backhanded / ✨ Prophecy / 🌀 Chaos) — since by reveal time the twist is already out, this is just flavor, not a spoiler.
